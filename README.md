# Murder in the Rue Cirque

<p align="center"><img width="251" height="125" alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0ad14b06-b5ae-4f7d-8c83-0e7c2361756a"/></p> 

<p align="center">The small traveling circus of Rue Cirque is thrown into turmoil when the brutal murder of Mr. Waddles is discovered in his locked wagon.</p>

<p align="center">With time running out before the circus moves on—potentially taking the killer with it—a detective is called to unravel the mystery. They have three days to examine the crime scene, gather evidence, and interrogate the circus members, piecing together the truth.</p>

<p align="center"><b>You can download an play the game yourself! Just visit:</b> https://ruecirque.itch.io/the-murder-in-the-rue-cirque</p>

<p align="center"><img width="251" height="125" alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2358b53f-275a-4399-b651-b93a15a797f2"/></p>

<p align="center">The Murder in the Rue Cirque is a rich, story-driven point-and-click game. As the newly arrived detective, you have three days to investigate the crime scene, gather any sinister-looking objects hidden within the suspiciously large wagon that might be linked to the murder of the late Mr. Waddles, and confront your suspects.</p>


<p align="center"><img width="1904" height="1067" alt="grafik" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4576b6ed-194d-481b-9941-90154b3775f4"/></p>


<p align="center">Your greatest weapons? A suitcase full of evidence and a notebook brimming with details everyone else has overlooked.</p>
